Heavy-Duty Swing Stand

Crossed-arch swing stand on a 4-piece ring base. 92 in tall, 105 in ring. Rated for a 350 lb rider, 1,050 lb peak hanging load.

Drawing 1: elevation

Drawing 2: plan

Drawing 3: arm for the roller

  • Arms: 3 in Sch 40 pipe, bend radius 46-15/16 in to the centerline, straight leg 37-7/16 in, 90 degree bend.
  • Ring quarters: 3x3x3/16 tube, radius 50-15/16 in to the centerline, 90 degrees each, 72 in straight across end to end. Cut the ends square to the curve.

Drawing 4: foot splice

Drill the ring through the clamped AF. Sleeve each hole, welded flush top and bottom.

CNC plates

One DXF per plate, inches, centered on the origin. Layers: CUT and ETCH. Corners rounded. Break and grind all plate edges smooth.
